# Projects on Robinhood Chain

> Directory of protocols building on Robinhood Chain: lending, DEXs, perps, tokenized stocks, launchpads and more, with links and TVL.

_60 projects_

Directory of the protocols building on Robinhood Chain — lending, DEXs, perps, tokenized stocks,
launchpads, gaming and infrastructure — with their contracts, live transactions and stats.

## Lending & Credit

- [Longbow](https://hoodscan.co/project/longbow) ($BOW) — Non-custodial credit layer on Morpho: borrow USDG against stock tokens, memes, RWAs and NFTs.
- [Morpho Blue](https://hoodscan.co/project/morpho-blue) — Isolated lending markets; home of the STEAKUSDG and USDE vaults that hold most of the chain's TVL.
- [Steakhouse Financial](https://hoodscan.co/project/steakhouse-financial) — Risk curator running the largest Morpho vaults on the chain.
- [Accountable](https://hoodscan.co/project/accountable) — Uncollateralized lending; USDE pool with a real base yield.
- [TermMax](https://hoodscan.co/project/termmax) — Fixed-rate, fixed-term lending.
- [Ripe Protocol](https://hoodscan.co/project/ripe-protocol) ($RIPE) — Lending protocol where a whole portfolio — crypto, yield positions, tokenized stocks, NFTs — backs a single GREEN stablecoin loan; RIPE is the DAO governance token.

## DEXs

- [Uniswap](https://hoodscan.co/project/uniswap) — V2, V3 and V4 deployments; V4 hosts most stock/USDG pairs and the Umia launch.
- [up](https://hoodscan.co/project/up) ($UP) — Native DEX with V2 and V3 pools.
- [Ramses](https://hoodscan.co/project/ramses) ($RAM) — ve(3,3) DEX with concentrated liquidity, DLMM and legacy pools.
- [Fables](https://hoodscan.co/project/fables) — DEX focused on the chain's stock and meme pairs.
- [SushiSwap](https://hoodscan.co/project/sushiswap) — SushiSwap V3 deployment.
- [Ekubo](https://hoodscan.co/project/ekubo) — Gas-efficient AMM; runs the STONX pools and high-emission stock/USDG farms.
- [GIGA](https://hoodscan.co/project/giga) — DEX with V2 and V3 pools.
- [Alandale](https://hoodscan.co/project/alandale) — DEX running the highest-emission USDG-LUTE and WETH-NVDA farms.
- [Curve](https://hoodscan.co/project/curve) — Curve stableswap deployment.
- [SectorOne](https://hoodscan.co/project/sectorone) — DLMM-style DEX.
- [Deepstate](https://hoodscan.co/project/deepstate) — On-chain order book for stock tokens, with DEEP emissions.
- [Catnip](https://hoodscan.co/project/catnip) — Small DEX; no website found yet.
- [BrownFi](https://hoodscan.co/project/brownfi) — AMM with a V3 deployment.
- [Orvex](https://hoodscan.co/project/orvex) — MetaDEX with v2 and v4 liquidity, smart routing and veORVX governance aimed at RWA trading.
- [Kipseli](https://hoodscan.co/project/kipseli) — Signed-quote PropAMM by Kipseli Capital, also on Base and BNB.
- [route.fun](https://hoodscan.co/project/route-fun) — Swap front end that races its own routing against KyberSwap and settles with no fee of its own.

## Perps & Leverage

- [LONG()](https://hoodscan.co/project/long) — LongX wraps leveraged Lighter positions into tradeable ERC-20 spot tokens such as NVDA3x.
- [Lighter](https://hoodscan.co/project/lighter) — Perps venue for tokenized stocks and crypto; LONG() wraps its positions.
- [Arcus](https://hoodscan.co/project/arcus) — Perps plus pTokens (tokenized perp exposure).
- [Meridian](https://hoodscan.co/project/meridian) — Perps venue plus a prediction market.

## Yield & Vaults

- [EARN](https://hoodscan.co/project/earn) ($EARN) — Agent-managed concentrated-liquidity vaults for tokenized stock pairs like NVDA, SPY and SPCX, with fee compounding.
- [Spark Savings](https://hoodscan.co/project/spark-savings) — USDG savings with a real base yield, backed by Sky.
- [Snuggle](https://hoodscan.co/project/snuggle) — Automated liquidity manager for concentrated-liquidity positions.
- [Krystal](https://hoodscan.co/project/krystal) — AI-agent auto-farm vault for LP positions.
- [Revert Finance](https://hoodscan.co/project/revert-finance) — LP management for Uniswap v3 and v4 positions: swap-and-mint, range changes, fee compounding, one-token exits.
- [D2 Finance](https://hoodscan.co/project/d2-finance) — Onchain capital allocator running discretionary strategies.
- [ARMSys](https://hoodscan.co/project/armsys) — Uniswap v4 dynamic-fee hook that protects LPs from loss-versus-rebalancing.
- [Delta](https://hoodscan.co/project/delta) — Liquidity manager.
- [Arcadia Finance](https://hoodscan.co/project/arcadia-finance) — Cross-margin accounts that hold a whole portfolio as collateral and manage LP positions on it, with up to 10x the capital.

## Tokenized Stocks & RWA

- [NetNetCap](https://hoodscan.co/project/netnetcap) ($NET) — OHM-style reserve token whose treasury holds tokenized stocks and USDG; 60-second long/short minigames and discounted bonds.
- [Prism Assets](https://hoodscan.co/project/prism-assets) ($PRISM) — AI-assisted marketplace to tokenize and trade RWAs; thousands of assets and baskets, fees flow to PRISM holders.
- [The Index](https://hoodscan.co/project/the-index) ($INDEX) — Stock dividend protocol: a 3% trade tax buys stock tokens that are airdropped to holders every 15 minutes.
- [Hood Index](https://hoodscan.co/project/hood-index) ($M7) — 1:1 collateralized MAG7 stock-token index priced by Chainlink; fees burn M7. Announcement stage.

## Launchpads

- [Pons](https://hoodscan.co/project/pons) ($PONS) — No-code fair-launch launchpad; 80% of fees buy back and burn PONS. Longbow and Stock Miners launched on it.
- [NOXA Fun](https://hoodscan.co/project/noxa-fun) — Token launchpad; the largest by TVL on the chain.
- [StonkBrokers](https://hoodscan.co/project/stonkbrokers) ($STONKBROKER) — Launchpad by Clutch Markets.
- [Sentry](https://hoodscan.co/project/sentry) — Token launchpad, also active on Ink Chain.
- [Coinbarrel](https://hoodscan.co/project/coinbarrel) ($CB) — Launchpad migrated from Solana; permanent Uniswap liquidity and creator fee share.
- [ArrowPad](https://hoodscan.co/project/arrowpad) — Pump.fun-style launchpad with fixed 1B supply and liquidity locked in Uniswap V3. Announcement stage.

## Gaming & Mining

- [StockRip](https://hoodscan.co/project/stockrip) — Gamified mining that pays out in tokenized stocks.
- [SLVR](https://hoodscan.co/project/slvr) — 5x5 grid lottery mining game with ETH prizes, a jackpot and staking fee-share.
- [STOCKMON](https://hoodscan.co/project/stockmon) — Stock-DNA creature NFTs whose IDs own onchain vault subledgers.

## Payments & Consumer

- [Mesh Gateway](https://hoodscan.co/project/mesh-gateway) ($MESH) — First MPP/x402 payment gateway on the chain: gasless for the payer, settles USDG here and USDC on Base.
- [Greenwood Finance](https://hoodscan.co/project/greenwood-finance) — Self-custody USDG smart-account neobank with a card, Morpho yield and Stockback equity rewards.
